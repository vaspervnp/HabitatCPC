@WELCOME TO HABITAT
YOU HAVE LANDED ON A BARREN MOON WITH A SHIP, SIX COLONISTS AND SOME
MATERIAL. IN FOURTEEN STEPS THIS TUTORIAL BUILDS A COLONY THAT LIVES. EACH
STEP OPENS WITH A PAGE LIKE THIS ONE, AND THE LINE UNDER THE BUTTONS SAYS IT
AGAIN IN SHORT. *?* SHOWS THE PAGE AGAIN.

*THE TOP ROWS*  *O2* *PWR* *H2O* *FOD* ARE OXYGEN, POWER, WATER AND FOOD.
A BAR TURNS RED BELOW ONE THIRD. *FE* IS METAL AND *BI* BIOPLASTIC: THE
BUILDINGS COST THEM. THEN THE COLONISTS, THE SOL (THE DAY NUMBER), DAY OR
NIGHT, AND THE ALERT LINE.

*THE MAP*  THE ARROWS MOVE THE CURSOR, ONE TILE A PRESS; HELD, IT KEEPS
GOING. THE VIEW FOLLOWS IT. *H* CENTRES THE VIEW ON THE SHIP.

*THE BUTTONS*  THE LETTER UNDER A BUTTON IS ITS KEY: *D* DOMES, *R* CORRIDOR,
*P* POWER, *E* RESOURCES, *T* TRANSPORT, *M* MANAGE. *ESC* GOES BACK.
*1* TO *4* SET THE SPEED: PAUSE, NORMAL, FAST AND VERY FAST.

UNTIL THE LAST STEP NOTHING GOES WRONG BY ITSELF: NO METEORS, NO STORMS AND
NO BROKEN MACHINES.
@STEP 1 OF 14: A SOLAR PANEL
EVERYTHING IN A COLONY RUNS ON POWER: THE AIR, THE WATER, THE FOOD AND THE
FACTORIES. A SOLAR PANEL MAKES POWER BY DAY.

  *P*         OPENS THE POWER GROUP ON THE BUTTON BAR
  *S*         PICKS THE SOLAR PANEL
  *ARROWS*    MOVE ITS GHOST: GREEN WHERE IT CAN GO, RED WHERE IT CANNOT.
              THE ROWS UNDER THE BUTTONS SHOW THE PRICE, AND WHY NOT
  *FIRE*      OR *SPACE* PLACES IT. *ESC* GOES BACK.

PUT IT ON OPEN GROUND NEAR THE SHIP. THE PRICE IS PAID AT ONCE; THEN THE
COLONISTS CARRY THE METAL OUT IN THEIR SUITS, AND AN ENGINEER BUILDS IT.

THE NEXT STEP COMES AS SOON AS THE PANEL IS PLACED.
@STEP 2 OF 14: AN OXYGEN DOME
THE COLONISTS LIVE AND BREATHE INSIDE DOMES. A SMALL DOME WITH AN OXYGEN
MACHINE MAKES THE AIR (*O2*). ONLY SMALL DOMES CAN HOLD ONE.

  *D* *S* *O*     DOMES, SMALL, OXYGEN. PLACE IT NEAR THE SHIP WITH *FIRE*.

A DOME IS BUILT EMPTY. TO PUT A MACHINE IN IT, FIT IT:

  *FIRE*        WITH THE CURSOR ON THE DOME OPENS IT (OR *M* *F*)
  *FIRE*        AGAIN PUTS THE OXYGEN MACHINE IN ITS SLOT. *ESC* CLOSES.

THE MACHINE IS PAID WHEN YOU FIT IT. COLONISTS CARRY IT IN, AND AN ENGINEER
INSTALLS IT. THE NEXT STEP COMES AS SOON AS IT IS FITTED.
@STEP 3 OF 14: AN AIRLOCK
AIRLOCKS ARE THE COLONY'S ONLY DOORS. WITHOUT ONE NOBODY CAN GO OUT TO THE
PANELS, THE WATER OR THE MINE.

  *T* *A*     TRANSPORT, AIRLOCK. PLACE IT BESIDE THE DOME WITH *FIRE*.

A CORRIDOR JOINS IT TO THE NEAREST DOME BY ITSELF. OUTSIDE, THE COLONISTS
WALK IN SUITS ALONG PATHS ON THE GROUND, AND COME BACK IN BEFORE THE AIR IN
THE SUIT RUNS OUT.
@STEP 4 OF 14: AN EXTRACTOR
THE COLONISTS DRINK WATER (*H2O*), AND THE OXYGEN MACHINE MAKES THE AIR
FROM WATER. AN EXTRACTOR PUMPS IT OUT OF THE GROUND.

  *E* *X*     RESOURCES, EXTRACTOR. IT MUST STAND WITHIN TWO TILES OF WATER,
              OR THE PANEL SAYS *NEEDS WATER NEARBY*.

EVERYTHING OUTSIDE IS JOINED TO THE NEAREST AIRLOCK OR STRUCTURE BY A PATH,
UP TO 12 TILES LONG. IF THE WATER IS FARTHER, BUILD TOWARDS IT FIRST.
@STEP 5 OF 14: A CANTEEN
COLONISTS EAT MEALS, AND MEALS ARE COOKED IN A CANTEEN.

  *D* *S* *C*     DOMES, SMALL, CANTEEN. PLACE IT NEAR THE OXYGEN DOME: A
                CORRIDOR JOINS EVERY DOME WITHIN REACH, UP TO FOUR.
  *FIRE*        ON THE CANTEEN, AND *FIRE* AGAIN FITS *MEALS*.

THE SHIP BROUGHT FOOD (*FOD*) FOR THE FIRST DAYS. LATER THE MEALS ARE
COOKED FROM WHAT THE GREENHOUSE AND THE LAB GROW.
@STEP 6 OF 14: ANOTHER SOLAR PANEL
ONE PANEL IS NOT ENOUGH FOR THE OXYGEN MACHINE, THE EXTRACTOR AND THE
CANTEEN TOGETHER. WATCH *PWR*: WHEN THERE IS TOO LITTLE POWER, MACHINES
STOP, AND THE ALERT LINE SAYS SO.

  *P* *S*     ANOTHER SOLAR PANEL, NEAR THE FIRST.
@STEP 7 OF 14: A COLLECTOR
SOLAR PANELS MAKE NOTHING AT NIGHT (*NGT* ON THE TOP ROW). A COLLECTOR
STORES POWER BY DAY AND GIVES IT BACK AT NIGHT.

  *P* *C*     POWER, COLLECTOR.
@STEP 8 OF 14: A MINE
METAL (*FE*) PAYS FOR EVERY BUILDING AND MACHINE. IT IS MADE FROM ORE, AND
A MINE DIGS THE ORE.

  *E* *M*     RESOURCES, MINE. ON AN ORE VEIN IT DIGS AT FULL SPEED.
              ELSEWHERE AT HALF, AND THE PANEL SAYS *NO VEIN  HALF ORE*.

THE FIRST MINE IS FREE. COLONISTS CARRY THE ORE TO THE SHIP, OR LATER TO
A STORAGE DOME.
@STEP 9 OF 14: A FACTORY
A FACTORY TURNS ORE INTO METAL.

  *D* *S* *F*     DOMES, SMALL, FACTORY.
  *FIRE*        ON IT, AND *FIRE* AGAIN FITS *METAL*.

WHEN YOU FIT A DOME, *UP* AND *DOWN* PICK WHAT GOES IN THE SLOT: A FACTORY
CAN ALSO MAKE BIOPLASTIC, SPARES AND MORE.
@STEP 10 OF 14: A GREENHOUSE
FOOD GROWS IN A GREENHOUSE. A MEDIUM DOME HAS FOUR SLOTS.

  *D* *M* *G*     DOMES, MEDIUM, GREENHOUSE.
  *FIRE*        ON IT TO FIT IT:
  *LEFT* *RIGHT*  PICK A SLOT, *UP* *DOWN* A PLANT, AND *FIRE* PLANTS IT.
  *SPACE*       PUTS THE SAME PLANT IN EVERY SLOT.

PLANT STARCH AND VEGETABLES: THE CANTEEN COOKS THEM INTO MEALS.
@STEP 11 OF 14: A LAB
MEAT IS THE THIRD THING A MEAL CAN BE COOKED FROM. A LAB GROWS IT.

  *D* *S* *B*     DOMES, SMALL, LAB.
  *FIRE*        ON IT, AND *FIRE* AGAIN FITS *MEAT*.
@STEP 12 OF 14: A CONTROL ROOM
MORE COLONISTS COME ONLY TO A COLONY WITH A CONTROL ROOM: IT TALKS TO THE
SHIPS, AND EACH ONE RAISES THE NUMBER OF COLONISTS THE COLONY CAN HOLD.

  *D* *S* *N*     DOMES, SMALL, CONTROL.
@STEP 13 OF 14: A LANDING PAD
THE SHIPS LAND ON A PAD.

  *T* *P*     TRANSPORT, LANDING PAD. IT IS LARGE: FIND OPEN GROUND FOR IT.
@STEP 14 OF 14: CALL A SHIP
NOW THE COLONY CAN GROW.

  *C*         OPENS THE SHIP'S LINE: WHO WILL COME, AND WHAT THEY WILL DO.
  *FIRE*      CALLS THE SHIP. IT LANDS ON THE PAD A FEW SECONDS LATER.

NEW COLONISTS NEED BEDS: BUILD QUARTERS (*D* *S* *Q*) SOON.
@THE COLONY IS YOURS
THE TUTORIAL IS OVER, AND FROM NOW ON IT IS A NORMAL GAME.

MACHINES WEAR AND BREAK: ENGINEERS MEND THEM WITH SPARES (*SP*). FROM TWENTY
COLONISTS, METEORS, DUST STORMS AND SOLAR FLARES CAN COME, AND RAIDERS.
MERCHANTS AND VISITORS LAND ON THE PAD TOO.

KEEP AN EYE ON THE FOUR BARS AND ON THE ALERT LINE. *I* SAYS WHAT IS UNDER
THE CURSOR. *M* *S* SAVES THE GAME, *M* *L* LOADS IT.

*?* SHOWS WHAT EVERY BUTTON DOES.

@MILESTONE: FOOTHOLD
TEN COLONISTS, AND EVERY NEED GREEN FOR A WHOLE SOL: THE COLONY HAS A
FOOTHOLD ON THIS WORLD.

EACH MILESTONE ADDS TEN TO THE COLONY'S PRESTIGE. PRESTIGE BRINGS VISITORS,
AND THEY PAY IN COINS, WHICH THE MERCHANTS TAKE LIKE ANY OTHER GOOD.

*NEXT: INDUSTRY*
METAL, BIOPLASTIC AND SPARES MADE IN THE COLONY. A FACTORY DOME WITH THE
*METAL*, *PLASTIC* AND *SPARES* MACHINES MAKES THEM: ORE INTO METAL, STARCH
INTO BIOPLASTIC, AND THE TWO TOGETHER INTO SPARES.

*G* SHOWS ALL FIVE MILESTONES, AND HOW FAR THE COLONY IS FROM EACH.
@MILESTONE: INDUSTRY
METAL, BIOPLASTIC AND SPARES ARE MADE IN THE COLONY: IT CAN BUILD AND MEND
BY ITSELF.

*NEXT: INDEPENDENCE*
ALL TEN GOODS MADE HERE: WATER, FOOD, ORE, METAL, BIOPLASTIC, PROCESSORS,
SPARES, MEDICINE, GUNS AND BOTS. AND NO DEAL WITH A MERCHANT FOR FIVE SOLS
IN A ROW.

*G* SHOWS ALL FIVE MILESTONES, AND HOW FAR THE COLONY IS FROM EACH.
@MILESTONE: INDEPENDENCE
EVERY GOOD THE COLONY USES IS MADE HERE, AND IT HAS NOT TRADED FOR FIVE
SOLS: IT NO LONGER NEEDS THE SHIPS.

*NEXT: AUTOMATION*
EIGHT BOTS. THE *ROBOTS* MACHINE IN A FACTORY MAKES THEM FROM METAL,
PROCESSORS AND BIOPLASTIC, AND THEY WAKE BY THEMSELVES TO BUILD, CARRY AND
DIG.

*AND HABITAT*
80 COLONISTS, STILL INDEPENDENT, AND FIVE SOLS IN A ROW WITHOUT A DEATH.

*G* SHOWS ALL FIVE MILESTONES, AND HOW FAR THE COLONY IS FROM EACH.
@MILESTONE: AUTOMATION
EIGHT BOTS WORK IN THE COLONY. THEY NEITHER EAT NOR BREATHE: THEY BUILD,
CARRY THE GOODS AND DIG THE ORE, AND LEAVE THE MACHINES TO THE COLONISTS.

*THE LAST ONE IS HABITAT*
80 COLONISTS, A COLONY THAT MAKES ALL TEN GOODS AND HAS NOT TRADED FOR
FIVE SOLS, AND FIVE SOLS IN A ROW WITHOUT A DEATH.

*G* SHOWS ALL FIVE MILESTONES, AND HOW FAR THE COLONY IS FROM EACH.
@MILESTONE: HABITAT
80 COLONISTS, A COLONY THAT MAKES ALL IT NEEDS, AND FIVE SOLS IN A ROW
WITHOUT A DEATH: THIS WORLD IS A HOME NOW.

THAT WAS THE LAST MILESTONE. THERE IS NO END: THE COLONY GOES ON FOR AS
LONG AS IT LIVES, AND IT CAN GROW TO 160.

*G* SHOWS ALL FIVE MILESTONES.
@MILESTONES
THE COLONY IS COUNTED ONCE A SOL. EACH MILESTONE ADDS TEN TO ITS PRESTIGE,
AND PRESTIGE BRINGS VISITORS WHO PAY.

*FOOTHOLD*      ^0  TEN COLONISTS, AND EVERY NEED GREEN FOR A WHOLE SOL
                    NOW ^A COLONISTS, ^B SOLS ALL GREEN

*INDUSTRY*      ^1  METAL, BIOPLASTIC AND SPARES MADE IN THE COLONY
                    NOW ^C OF THE THREE

*INDEPENDENCE*  ^2  ALL TEN GOODS MADE HERE, AND NO TRADE FOR FIVE SOLS
                    NOW ^D OF THE TEN, ^E SOLS WITHOUT TRADE

*AUTOMATION*    ^3  EIGHT BOTS
                    NOW ^F BOTS

*HABITAT*       ^4  80 COLONISTS, INDEPENDENT, AND FIVE SOLS IN A ROW
                    WITHOUT A DEATH. NOW ^G SOLS WITHOUT A DEATH

*SOL* ^H     *PRESTIGE* ^I     *COINS* ^J     *MOST COLONISTS* ^K
@COLONY LOST
EVERYONE IN THE COLONY IS DEAD.

  SOLS              ^H
  MOST COLONISTS    ^K
  PRESTIGE          ^I
  COINS             ^J
  MILESTONES        ^L OF FIVE

  *FOOTHOLD*        ^0
  *INDUSTRY*        ^1
  *INDEPENDENCE*    ^2
  *AUTOMATION*      ^3
  *HABITAT*         ^4

A SAVED GAME TAKES THE COLONY BACK TO WHERE IT WAS SAVED. A NEW COLONY
STARTS FROM THE MENU, AND THE GAME'S DISC MUST BE IN THE DRIVE FOR IT.
