# Habitat

## Description

Six colonists, one ship, an empty world. Habitat is a colony-survival game for the Amstrad CPC 6128. Place domes, airlocks and machines on a planet generated for you, then watch your colonists walk out, take the work you offer and keep the oxygen running on their own. Balance power, water and fourteen goods, ride out storms, solar flares, meteors and intruders, trade with merchants, and grow from a foothold into a true habitat of up to 160 people.

## Story

Nobody chose this world for its beauty. The survey drones found ice under the dust, ore in the valleys and a sky thin enough to kill in three minutes. That was enough.

The ship came down at dawn with six people aboard and a hold of metal and bioplastic. No base was waiting and no orders would come from home. The only other visitors would be merchants, crossing the system every few sols, if they still came.

The first dome went up beside the ship, and then an airlock, because outside the suits held three minutes of air and not a breath more. Solar panels turned toward a weak sun. Pumps bit into the ice. An oxygen generator began, slowly, to make the air they would all share.

Nobody gave orders. The engineer fixed what broke. The biologist planted starch and vegetables under glass. The workers carried metal through the dust, ten pieces at a time. When night fell and the panels went dark, they learned to store power. When the first sandstorm came, they learned to stay inside.

Sols passed. Ships brought newcomers who had heard there was air to breathe here. Merchants landed to trade. Visitors paid to see the colony that should not have survived. Then one evening a ship landed that had not come to trade, and the guards were ready.

In the end they took the old ship apart, plate by plate, and carried it into storage. It had brought them here, and it would not take them home. This was home now.

They gave it the only name it could have.

Habitat.
